My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, and education. I investigate how data-driven methods can deepen our understanding of learning, support educational decision-making, and contribute to more effective educational systems.

Drawing on educational data mining, learning analytics, and machine learning, I study learning processes using large-scale assessment data. My current work focuses on LLM-augmented frameworks for forecasting international assessment trends, with applications to PISA and related programmes.

I am particularly interested in international large-scale assessments, AI in education, and the use of large language models for evidence synthesis in educational research. In parallel, I explore how machine learning can be taught effectively to students who are not computer science majors.
