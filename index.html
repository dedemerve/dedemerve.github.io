01About

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, and education. I investigate how data-driven methods can deepen our understanding of learning, support educational decision-making, and contribute to more effective educational systems.

Drawing on educational data mining, learning analytics, and machine learning, I study learning processes using large-scale assessment data. My work focuses on developing rigorous, interpretable, and reproducible methods for analyzing complex educational datasets.

I am particularly interested in international large-scale assessments, AI in education, and the use of large language models for educational research. In parallel, I explore how machine learning can be taught effectively to non-majors.

03Research Areas
Educational Data Mining
Machine learning and statistical modeling applied to educational data, uncovering patterns that inform instruction, policy, and system design. EDM treats educational datasets as complex, hierarchically structured sources of evidence.
Learning Analytics
Measurement, collection, and analysis of learner data to understand and optimize learning — bridging educational research and data science, connecting large-scale assessment findings to actionable evidence.
LLMs for Evidence Synthesis
Schema-constrained LLMs for extracting structured evidence from research literature at scale, enabling systematic, auditable synthesis of findings across thousands of studies into queryable knowledge bases.
International Large-Scale Assessments
Secondary analysis of PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS, TALIS, PIAAC, ICILS, and ICCS. Valid analysis requires careful attention to plausible values, replicate weights, and complex sampling designs.
Machine Learning Education
Design and evaluation of curricula for ML competencies in non-CS majors — including preservice teachers and education faculty — in contexts where ML is becoming methodologically relevant.
Explainable AI in Education
Model interpretability via SHAP and LIME, making predictive educational models transparent and actionable for practitioners who make decisions based on model outputs.
